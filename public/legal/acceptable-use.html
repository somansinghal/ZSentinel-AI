Acceptable Use Policy

Last Updated: September 27, 2026 • Version: 1.0 (Datathon Edition)
Important Hackathon Notice: These documents are provided for this hackathon/demo project (IBM Z Datathon 2026) and should be reviewed by a qualified legal professional before being used as production legal terms. Z-Sentinel AI is an educational demonstration prototype.

1. Purpose & Educational Context

This Acceptable Use Policy defines the parameters for interacting with the Z-Sentinel AI demonstration platform. The system is designed to showcase modern cybersecurity techniques, anomaly detection, and privacy protection for IBM Z architectures.

2. Controlled Simulation Features

The platform includes interactive attack simulation buttons (such as "Simulate Attack Pattern", "Generate Transaction Burst", and "Simulate Account Takeover"). These capabilities:

  • Are strictly simulated in-browser or via harmless synthetic payload generators.
  • Do not perform real cyberattacks, port scanning, or network intrusion against external hosts or actual IBM mainframes.
  • Are provided solely for hackathon judges and evaluators to observe the response of the rule engine and ML model.

3. Prohibited Activities

Users, judges, and evaluators are strictly prohibited from:

  • Malicious Penetration Testing: Executing automated fuzzing, distributed denial of service (DDoS), or vulnerability exploitation against the hosting servers or backend APIs.
  • Credential & Secret Harvesting: Attempting to reverse engineer, inspect memory dumps, or extract private environment variables (such as backend API keys or service tokens).
  • Real PII Injection: Inserting genuine payment card details, social security numbers, or confidential corporate credentials into chat prompts or simulation inputs.
  • Impersonation & Tampering: Tampering with authentication headers to impersonate other real users.

4. Reporting Vulnerabilities

If you discover a security vulnerability within the demonstration codebase, please review our Security Policy and report it responsibly without public disclosure.

Cookie & Local Storage Policy

Last Updated: September 27, 2026 • Version: 1.0 (Datathon Edition)
Important Hackathon Notice: These documents are provided for this hackathon/demo project (IBM Z Datathon 2026) and should be reviewed by a qualified legal professional before being used as production legal terms. Z-Sentinel AI is an educational demonstration prototype.

1. Overview

Z-Sentinel AI respects your privacy and is committed to minimal data persistence. We do not employ invasive tracking cookies, third-party marketing beacons, or cross-site fingerprinting scripts.

2. Technologies We Actually Use

Our platform uses only strictly necessary browser storage mechanisms to provide essential functions:

  • Firebase Authentication Tokens (IndexedDB / Session Storage): Managed directly by the Google Firebase Web SDK to persist your authenticated login state securely across page reloads.
  • HTML5 Local Storage (localStorage):
    • zsentinel_judge_demo_mode: Stores a boolean flag indicating if Judge Demo Mode is active.
    • zsentinel_demo_user: Stores the synthetic profile used in demo evaluations.
    • zsentinel_firebase_config: Stores optional custom client-side Firebase web configuration if you explicitly enter it via the settings dialog.

3. Third-Party Analytics

This demonstration platform does not utilize third-party advertising or commercial analytics trackers (such as Google Analytics or Facebook Pixel). CDN assets (such as Lucide Icons and Chart.js) are loaded to deliver icons and client-side graphs without setting persistent tracking cookies.

4. Managing and Clearing Storage

You can remove all stored session preferences at any time by clearing your browser's site data, clicking "Logout" in the navigation topbar, or clicking "Exit Demo Mode".

AI Transparency & Disclaimer

Last Updated: September 27, 2026 • Version: 1.0 (Datathon Edition)
Important Hackathon Notice: These documents are provided for this hackathon/demo project (IBM Z Datathon 2026) and should be reviewed by a qualified legal professional before being used as production legal terms. Z-Sentinel AI is an educational demonstration prototype.

1. Artificial Intelligence Architecture Overview

Z-Sentinel AI employs a layered intelligence system consisting of:

  • Deterministic Security Heuristics: Rule-based deduction points evaluating device novelty, impossible travel vectors, velocity spikes, and amount deviations.
  • Unsupervised Machine Learning: Scikit-learn Isolation Forest algorithms trained on multi-dimensional transaction features to compute continuous anomaly scores.
  • Local Explanation Engine: Deterministic natural language synthesis explaining the specific indicators that produced the risk score.
  • Optional Groq AI (Server-Side Fallback): Large Language Model queries for natural language summarization and analyst question-answering.

2. Non-Infallibility & Probabilistic Nature

Machine learning algorithms and Large Language Models are probabilistic by design:

  • Anomaly scores and risk percentages (0–100) indicate statistical likelihoods, not infallible absolute truth.
  • False positives (legitimate transactions flagged as anomalous) and false negatives (anomalous transactions appearing benign) may occur.
  • AI Copilot natural language responses are generated for assistive operational support and should be verified against raw audit trails.

3. Requirement for Human Oversight ("Human-in-the-Loop")

In mission-critical IBM Z mainframe banking and enterprise workflows, automated decisions (such as transaction holds, fraud containment, or account freezes) should serve as decision-support alerts. Final security containment and legal actions must always remain under the authority of a qualified human security analyst or audited institutional procedure.

4. Data Minimization Before LLM Inference

To safeguard consumer privacy, the platform strictly enforces automated local PII redaction. No raw Primary Account Numbers, Social Security Numbers, or personal credentials are sent to external LLMs.

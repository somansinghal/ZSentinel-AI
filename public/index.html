The Challenge

Mainframe Cyber Threats Demand Smarter Guardians

IBM Z powers over 70% of global financial transactions. However, traditional legacy SIEMs and rigid signature systems struggle against modern distributed stealth attacks, credential stuffing, and unmasked data pipelines.

Legacy Mainframe Security Gaps

  • Rigid Static Thresholds: Fails to detect coordinated low-and-slow account takeovers and complex behavioral deviations.
  • Unprotected Data Exposure: PAN, SSN, and banking IDs frequently leak into audit streams, violating GDPR and PCI-DSS.
  • Fragile Cloud-AI Dependencies: Standard cloud AI APIs introduce latency, rate limits, and failure risks unsuitable for mission-critical core banking.

The Z-Sentinel AI Breakthrough

  • Multi-Vector ML Detection: Real-time Isolation Forest algorithms analyze velocity, deviation, and device entropy dynamically.
  • Zero-Leakage Privacy Guardian: Deterministic masking engine redacts PII at the ingestion boundary before any storage or AI query.
  • Resilient Local Core: Security decisions are 100% deterministic and locally calculated, with Groq AI serving as an optional natural language explainer.
